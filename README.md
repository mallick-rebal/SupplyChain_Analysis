# SupplyChain_Analysis

An end-to-end supply-chain analytics platform designed to identify
fulfillment bottlenecks, profitability issues, product performance
patterns, and operational priorities.

## Business Problem

Operations management needs visibility into:

- where deliveries are failing;
- which markets and products are most affected;
- how fulfillment issues affect revenue and profit;
- which products and categories perform best;
- how demand changes over time;
- where management should focus operational attention.
